What we do

We find the work worth automating — then automate it with agents.

Process discovery

Gen AI-led interviews with your process owners map how work really gets done, then score every process for automation readiness and ROI.

Agentic RPA

Reliable RPA bots for the rule-based steps, AI agents for the judgment calls. One automation that handles the whole process, exceptions included.

Gen AI solutions

Focused Gen AI tools for the business problems that don't fit a bot: document understanding, research, drafting and decision support.
